Privacy Policy

Effective date: 09/15/2026 · Last updated: 09/15/2026

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if used for SMS reminders)

Information collected automatically:

  • Which anchors/moments you select and use (e.g. Laundry, Coffee)
  • App usage patterns (e.g. session completions, frequency of use)
  • Device type and operating system, for compatibility and troubleshooting

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera access unless a future feature explicitly requires it and asks for your permission separately.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to personalize your experience, send reminders (if you've opted in), understand how people use Herely so we can improve it, and communicate important updates about the App.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the App (e.g. hosting, analytics, notification delivery), under confidentiality obligations, or with legal authorities if required by law.

4. Data Storage & Security

Herely does not operate a server or cloud backend. Your information, including which moments you've added and your listening history, is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded anywhere. Your device's own encryption (active automatically when your phone has a passcode set) protects this information. Deleting the App, or your data within it, removes it immediately, since no copy exists anywhere else. If Herely later introduces account sync or other features that require storing data on a server, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what changes.

5. Your Rights

You can access the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, via Settings or by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Herely is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to access/delete your data, can be sent to hello@beherely.com.
